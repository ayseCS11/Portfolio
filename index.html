AI research

Simulation based research using computer vision and deep reinforcement learning.

Automatic Traffic Lights Control System (ATLCS I)

My final year research project at GCWUS. It optimizes traffic signal timing so vehicles wait less at intersections.

  • YOLOv4 trained on 97 images across 6 vehicle classes, reaching an mAP of 0.53549
  • A DQN reinforcement learning agent trained in the SUMO traffic simulator over 100 episodes
  • Average waiting time of 0.32 seconds and average queue length of 52.51 vehicles
  • Outperformed the FCMQN+MP, FLC, and OATS baselines
YOLOv4DQNSUMOPython
